# Standard Terms of Business for Panel Appointments

Synthetic demo document.

## 1 Liability

The firm accepts a liability cap of £50 million per claim for panel appointments, matching its professional indemnity insurance cover.

## 2 Termination

Either party may terminate the framework on 30 days' written notice, with live matters transitioned in an orderly way at the client's direction.

## 3 Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 4 Audit Rights

The client may audit the firm's billing records for panel matters on 10 business days' notice, no more than once per year unless a discrepancy is found.

## 5 Subcontracting

The firm does not subcontract panel work without the client's prior written consent, and remains responsible for any agreed subcontractor's work.
