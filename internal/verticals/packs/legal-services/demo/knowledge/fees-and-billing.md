# Panel Fees and Billing Arrangements 2026

Synthetic demo document. All rates are fictional and exclusive of VAT.

## 1 Rate Card

Proposed hourly rates by grade of fee earner for panel work are: partner £495, senior associate £360, associate £275 and trainee £150 per hour.

## 2 Panel Discount

The firm offers a 15% discount against its standard rates for all panel work, applied automatically on every invoice.

## 3 Alternative Fee Arrangements

Alternative fee arrangements offered include fixed fees for defined scopes, capped fees with a written cap agreed before work starts, a blended rate of £310 per hour across all grades, and annual retainers for advisory work.

## 4 Billing

Invoices are issued monthly in arrears with narrative time entries, and the firm supports e-billing in LEDES format through Tymetrix and Legal Tracker, with invoices payable within 30 days.

## 5 Budgets and Estimates

Every matter receives a written budget or estimate at the outset, tracked in the matter management system, and the client is notified in writing before any estimate is exceeded by more than 10%, with a variation agreed before further work is billed.

## 6 Disbursements

Disbursements are charged at cost with no mark-up, and third-party costs above £500 are agreed with the client in advance.
