# Halden & Crewe LLP - Firm Overview

Synthetic demo document describing a fictional UK commercial law firm.

## 1 Overview

Halden & Crewe LLP was established in 1994 and is a full-service commercial law firm with 42 partners and 310 fee earners in total. The firm advises corporate, infrastructure and public-sector clients across the United Kingdom.

## 2 Offices

Panel work is delivered from four UK offices: the London headquarters, Manchester, Leeds and Edinburgh. The firm works with independent alliance firms in Dublin and Frankfurt for matters governed by Irish or German law.

## 3 Financial Standing

Turnover for the financial year ending 30 April 2025 was £98 million, the third consecutive year of growth. Audited accounts are available to prospective clients on request, and the firm carries no external debt beyond a revolving facility used for working capital.

## 4 Regulation and Accreditations

The firm is authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA). It has held the Law Society's Lexcel practice management accreditation since 2012 and holds ISO 27001 certification for information security together with Cyber Essentials certification.

## 5 Ownership and Structure

Halden & Crewe is a limited liability partnership that is wholly partner-owned with no external investors. Profit sharing is on a modified lockstep, and the firm is governed by an elected management board chaired by the managing partner.
