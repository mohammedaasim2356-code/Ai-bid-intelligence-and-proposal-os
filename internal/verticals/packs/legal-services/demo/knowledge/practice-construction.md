# Practice Note: Construction, Infrastructure and Procurement

Synthetic demo document.

## 1 Infrastructure and Construction Projects

The construction and infrastructure team advises on the procurement and delivery of rail, water and energy projects under NEC4 and JCT contract forms, including bespoke amendments, design and build arrangements and framework call-offs. The team has advised on projects with a combined capital value above £2 billion in the last three years.

## 2 Public Procurement

The team advises contracting authorities and bidders on public procurement, including procedures under the Procurement Act 2023 and the Public Contracts Regulations 2015, standstill challenges and framework design.

## 3 Construction Disputes

The construction disputes team handles adjudication, mediation and Technology and Construction Court proceedings. In the last 24 months the team has handled 38 adjudications as referring or responding party, with the majority concerning payment and extension of time.

## 4 Planning and Environment

The team advises on planning and environmental consents for linear infrastructure, including development consent orders, environmental impact assessment and habitats regulations assessment.
