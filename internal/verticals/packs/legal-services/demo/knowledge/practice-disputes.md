# Practice Note: Disputes and Arbitration

Synthetic demo document.

## 1 Commercial Litigation

The disputes team conducts commercial litigation in the High Court and the Court of Appeal, including contractual, shareholder and professional negligence claims.

## 2 International Arbitration

The team has acted as counsel in ICC and LCIA arbitrations seated in London, Paris and Geneva, including energy and construction disputes with values between £5 million and £400 million.

## 3 Mediation and Alternative Dispute Resolution

The firm has accredited mediators among its partners and resolves the majority of disputes before trial through mediation, early neutral evaluation and negotiated settlement.

## 4 Regulatory Investigations

The team advises regulated utilities on investigations and enforcement by economic and environmental regulators.
