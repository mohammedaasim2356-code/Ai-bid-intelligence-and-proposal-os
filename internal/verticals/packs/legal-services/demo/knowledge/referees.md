# Approved Client Referees

Synthetic demo document. Referees have consented to be contacted for panel tenders.

## 1 Available Referees

Two clients may be contacted as referees: Northgate Rail (Head of Legal, referee.northgate@example) for infrastructure and construction work, and Beacon Water Services (General Counsel, referee.beacon@example) for regulatory and disputes work.

## 2 Referee Protocol

Referees are contacted only after the client has been notified of the specific tender, and no more than three times per year.
