# Risk, Insurance, Information Security and Compliance Statement

Synthetic demo document.

## 1 Professional Indemnity Insurance

The firm holds professional indemnity insurance cover of £50 million for any one claim with a primary insurer rated A by AM Best; the policy renews on 1 October each year and the certificate is available on request.

## 2 Information Security

The firm is ISO 27001 certified for information security, holds Cyber Essentials certification, encrypts data at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher, and enforces multi-factor authentication for all remote access.

## 3 Data Protection

The firm complies with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, has an appointed Data Protection Officer, and hosts all client data in UK data centres operated under the firm's ISO 27001 scope.

## 4 Business Continuity

The business continuity plan is tested annually with a recovery time objective of 4 hours for core systems and a recovery point objective of 15 minutes.

## 5 Anti-money Laundering and Sanctions

Client due diligence and anti-money laundering checks are completed before any matter is opened, with sanctions screening of clients and counterparties against the UK and international sanctions lists repeated at intervals throughout the matter.

## 6 Claims and Regulatory History

There have been no successful professional negligence claims against the firm and no SRA disciplinary findings in the last five years.

## 7 Conflict Checks

Conflict checks are completed within 24 hours of a new instruction using the firm's central conflicts database, and information barriers are put in place where the firm acts for parties with potentially adverse interests, with the barrier documented and monitored by the risk team.
