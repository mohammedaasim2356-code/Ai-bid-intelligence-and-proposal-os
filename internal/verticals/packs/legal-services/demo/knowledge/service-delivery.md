# Client Service Standards

Synthetic demo document.

## 1 Response Times

The firm acknowledges every new instruction within 2 hours and gives a substantive response within 24 hours during working hours; an out-of-hours partner line is available for urgent matters.

## 2 Reporting

Clients receive monthly matter reports covering progress, spend against budget and next steps, quarterly panel review meetings with the relationship partner, and an annual relationship review with the client's general counsel.

## 3 Key Performance Indicators

The firm proposes twelve key performance indicators for panel work, including budget accuracy, response time compliance, invoice query rate, matter outcome against objectives and client satisfaction score, each measured from matter management and billing data and reported quarterly.

## 4 Secondments

The firm offers two secondees per year to panel clients at no charge, typically associates with two to four years of experience, for periods of three to six months.

## 5 Training and Know-how

The firm provides 10 training sessions per year at no additional charge, tailored to the client's teams, together with fortnightly know-how bulletins and access to the firm's precedent bank.

## 6 Complaints

The firm operates a written complaints handling procedure led by the client care partner, acknowledges complaints within two working days and responds substantively within 20 working days, with a right to refer unresolved complaints to the Legal Ombudsman.
