## 4 Leverage

The proposed leverage for panel matters is one partner to four associates, with a senior associate as day-to-day contact on every matter and trainees used only for supervised document review.

## 5 Continuity

Each named partner has a named deputy who is briefed on all live matters. The firm gives 30 days' written notice of any change to the named team and agrees replacements with the client before they take effect.

## 6 Supervision

All work by junior lawyers is supervised and reviewed by a partner or senior associate before it leaves the firm, in line with the firm's Lexcel supervision policy, and every matter has a supervising partner recorded in the matter management system.
