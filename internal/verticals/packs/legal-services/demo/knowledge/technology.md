# Technology and Innovation Overview

Synthetic demo document.

## 1 Matter Management and Client Extranet

Matters are managed in iManage with budget tracking, and clients access the HC Connect client extranet for live matter dashboards, document sharing and spend reporting.

## 2 Document Automation

The firm uses Contract Express document automation for standard documents such as non-disclosure agreements, board minutes and framework call-off contracts, reducing turnaround for routine documents to the same day.

## 3 Artificial Intelligence Policy

Generative AI tools are used for research and first drafts only under a written policy: every output receives human review by a qualified lawyer before use, client data is never entered into public models, and only tools within the firm's ISO 27001 security scope are permitted.

## 4 Legal Project Management

The firm employs 6 certified legal project managers who scope, plan and track large matters, and every panel matter above £100,000 in fees is assigned a legal project manager.
