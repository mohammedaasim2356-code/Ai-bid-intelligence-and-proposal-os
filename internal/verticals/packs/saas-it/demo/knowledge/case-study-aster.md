# Case Study: Aster Municipal Services Platform Consolidation

Synthetic demo document. Aster Municipal Services is a fictional public-sector customer.

## 1 Overview

Case study: Aster Municipal Services, a fictional regional public-sector body, consolidated three data centers onto Orbit in a cloud modernization program completed in 2025 within the last three years, covering 310 workloads and 4,200 users.

## 2 Challenge

Three inconsistent environments, duplicated licensing and an audit finding on disaster recovery readiness.

## 3 Solution

Wave-based migration with a single security baseline, centralized logging and a tested disaster recovery design.

## 4 Results

Results: data centers reduced from three to zero, disaster recovery test passed within the 4-hour objective, and licensing spend reduced by 22%.
