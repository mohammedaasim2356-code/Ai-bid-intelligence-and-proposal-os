# Case Study: Helios Retail Group Cloud Modernization

Synthetic demo document. Helios Retail Group is a fictional customer.

## 1 Overview

Case study: Helios Retail Group, a fictional omnichannel retailer, completed a cloud modernization program of similar scale to large enterprise migrations: 240 workloads migrated from two data centers to Orbit in 9 months during 2024 and 2025.

## 2 Challenge

Helios ran aging infrastructure with quarterly outages during peak trading and could not scale for seasonal demand.

## 3 Solution

Northstar delivered the program with the Launch Methodology in five waves, using Orbit Migrate for replication and cutover, with zero unplanned downtime during cutovers.

## 4 Results

Results: 31% reduction in infrastructure cost, 99.95% measured availability over the first 12 months on Orbit, and peak-season scaling completed automatically.
