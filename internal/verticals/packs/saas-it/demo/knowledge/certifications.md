# Certifications and Attestations

Synthetic demo document listing the attestations held by Northstar Cloud Systems.

## 1 SOC 2 Type II

Northstar holds a current SOC 2 Type II report covering the Security, Availability and Confidentiality trust services criteria. The most recent SOC 2 Type II report covers the audit period ending 31 March 2026 and was issued by an independent audit firm. Availability: a copy of the report is provided to prospective customers under NDA.

## 2 CSA STAR

Northstar publishes a CSA STAR Level 1 self-assessment (Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire) on the Cloud Security Alliance registry.

## 3 Cloud Provider Attestations

The hosting providers used by Orbit maintain their own independent attestations, which customers may rely on for physical and environmental controls.
