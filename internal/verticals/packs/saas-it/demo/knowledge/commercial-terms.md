# Commercial Terms and Licensing

Synthetic demo document summarizing standard commercial positions.

## 1 Pricing Model

Pricing model: Orbit is licensed as an annual subscription with a licensing metric of managed workloads (per workload per month) plus a platform fee per tenant. Volume discounts apply from 250 workloads (10%) and 500 workloads (18%). Professional services are priced per phase at fixed fees.

## 2 Payment Terms

Standard payment terms: subscription fees are invoiced annually in advance; professional services are invoiced monthly in arrears. Invoicing frequency can be changed to quarterly on request. Payment is due net 30 days from invoice date.

## 3 Contract Term and Termination

The standard contract term is three years with automatic one-year renewal terms. Termination for convenience is available after the first year with 90 days written notice; termination for cause is available at any time with a 30-day cure period.

## 4 Limitation of Liability

Limitation of liability position: liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim, with a higher cap of two times annual fees for data protection breaches and no cap for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 5 Insurance

Insurance coverage: cyber liability insurance of USD 10 million, professional indemnity (errors and omissions) insurance of USD 5 million and general liability insurance of USD 2 million. Certificates of insurance are available on request.

## 6 Warranties

Warranties: professional services are performed in a professional and workmanlike manner; the platform conforms to its documentation; non-conforming services are re-performed at no charge within a 30-day remedy period.
