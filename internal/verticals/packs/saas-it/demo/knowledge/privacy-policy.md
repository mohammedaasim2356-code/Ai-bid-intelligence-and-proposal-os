# Data Privacy and Protection Policy

Synthetic demo document describing how Northstar Cloud Systems protects personal data.

## 1 Regulatory Compliance

Northstar complies with GDPR and UK GDPR as a processor. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses is provided with every contract, and Northstar acts in accordance with the CCPA for California residents. A data protection officer is appointed.

## 2 Data Residency

Customer data is stored and processed only in the geographic regions selected by the customer: the EU (Ireland or Frankfurt, Germany), the United States (Virginia or Oregon) or Canada (Central). Data does not leave the selected region except for customer-initiated exports. Support staff access customer data only through audited just-in-time sessions.

## 3 Retention and Deletion

Data retention and deletion at contract termination: customer data is retained for 30 days after termination to allow export, then deleted from production and backups within a further 30 days. A certificate of deletion signed by the security team is provided on request.

## 4 Subprocessors

The current list of subprocessors is published on the Northstar trust page and includes the hosting providers (AWS, Microsoft Azure), Brightpath Email Services (transactional email) and Lumen Support Desk (support ticketing). Notification process for changes: customers are notified 30 days before a new subprocessor is added and may object in writing.

## 5 Data Subject Requests

Data subject access requests are supported within statutory timelines: the console includes self-service tooling to export, correct or delete a data subject's records, and the privacy team responds to customer requests within 10 business days so that the customer can meet its one-month GDPR deadline.

## 6 Privacy Governance

Privacy impact assessments are performed for every new feature that processes personal data, and the policy is reviewed annually.
