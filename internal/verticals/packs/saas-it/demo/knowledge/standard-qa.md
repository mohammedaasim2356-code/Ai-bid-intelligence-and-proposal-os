# Standard Q&A Library

Synthetic demo document of approved standard answers.

## 1 Sandbox Environment

Question: Do you provide a sandbox environment for integration testing? Answer: Yes. Every subscription includes a non-production sandbox environment for integration testing at no additional cost, refreshed from production configuration on request.

## 2 Single Sign-On

Question: Which identity providers are supported for single sign-on? Answer: Any SAML 2.0 identity provider, including Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Ping Identity.

## 3 Uptime History

Question: What availability has the platform achieved? Answer: Trailing 12-month measured availability across all tenants is 99.97%.

## 4 Data Ownership

Question: Who owns the data stored on the platform? Answer: The customer owns all customer data; Northstar processes it only to deliver the service.

## 5 Exit Assistance

Question: What happens at the end of the contract? Answer: Northstar provides exit assistance including full data export in open formats and up to 60 days of transition support.
