# Support and Service Level Agreement

Synthetic demo document summarizing Northstar support tiers and service levels.

## 1 Support Tiers and Coverage

Support tiers: Standard support covers business hours (08:00-18:00 local time, Monday to Friday) and Premier support provides 24x7 coverage for Severity 1 incidents. Contact channels are the support portal, email and telephone. Support engineers are located in Ireland, Canada and the United States.

## 2 Severity Definitions and Targets

Severity definitions with target response and resolution times: Severity 1 (production down) response within 15 minutes and resolution target 4 hours; Severity 2 (major degradation) response within 1 hour and resolution target 8 hours; Severity 3 (minor impact) response within 4 hours and resolution target 3 business days; Severity 4 (question or enhancement) response within 1 business day and resolution in a future release.

## 3 Availability Commitment

Northstar commits to a minimum monthly availability of 99.9% for the Orbit platform, measured per tenant. Service credits apply when the commitment is missed: 5% of the monthly fee below 99.9%, 10% below 99.5% and 25% below 99.0%. Trailing 12-month availability is reported in the console.

## 4 Maintenance Windows

Zero-downtime upgrades are the standard approach: routine upgrades use rolling deployments with no customer-facing downtime. Planned maintenance that requires downtime is notified 10 business days in advance and scheduled in the customer's agreed window; blackout-window options let customers block maintenance during peak periods. Emergency maintenance is communicated by email and status page.

## 5 Escalation Path

The escalation path for unresolved incidents has three named escalation contacts provided at onboarding: the Support Manager, the Director of Support and the Vice President of Customer Operations. Severity 1 incidents escalate automatically after 2 hours without resolution.

## 6 Customer Success

A named customer success manager is assigned for the duration of the contract and runs quarterly business reviews, adoption reporting and renewal planning.
