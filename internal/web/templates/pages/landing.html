Problem

Requirements get missed, answers are copied from stale decks, reviewers cannot tell what is verified, and the same questionnaire is answered from scratch every quarter.

Stance

Evidence-backed AI

Labels come from facts, not confidence scores

Evidence gate

A chunk qualifies only if it covers the requirement's salient terms and mentions its hard terms (numbers, acronyms, proper nouns). The union of qualified chunks must cover every hard term, or the answer stops at Insufficient evidence.

Claim verifier

Every factual sentence needs a citation marker; every number and hard term must appear in the cited chunk; lexical overlap must clear a threshold. Unsupported sentences are highlighted, counted and blocked by QA.

Provider router

Local model → configured provider → deterministic demo floor. Sensitivity gate, cache, token budgets, schema validation with one repair, logged fallbacks. The demo needs no keys.

Prompt-injection resistance

Buyer documents are untrusted data. Instruction lines are filtered at extraction and the synthetic RFP contains one to prove it.

QA

Export is a gate, not a button

Missing mandatory answers, weak or absent evidence, missing citations, placeholders, stale or expired sources, contradictions, unsupported sentences, unresolved SME tasks, missing attachments and sections — each finding links to its entity and blocks export until resolved or dismissed with a reason.

ROI model

Estimated operational savings, formula visible and editable

hours = drafted × baseline hours × draft saving + reused × baseline hours × reuse saving + bids × admin hours × 50%. Every input is a setting on the Analytics page; every output is labelled an estimate.

Security principles

Server-side authorization

Sessions, RBAC per action, organization scoping on every query, CSRF tokens, rate limits, upload validation.

Keys never reach the browser

Provider credentials live in the environment; every result is labelled with its provider mode.

Sensitivity gate

Internal or confidential data only goes to providers explicitly allowed for it; otherwise the local model or demo floor answers.
